At the core of CMC’s identity is a culture built on craftsmanship, integrity, and honoring accountability.

CMC delivers superior construction management services throughout New York and beyond. Our experienced team brings decades of expertise to every project, ensuring exceptional results that meet the highest standards of quality and craftsmanship. Now strengthened by our partnership with Shimizu Corporation, we combine local knowledge with global capabilities while maintaining the personal attention that has defined us since 2003.

Since our founding in 2003, we’ve cultivated a team of professionals who approach construction not just as a process, but as an art that demands precision and care. We measure success not only by completed structures, but by the lasting partnerships we build with clients who return to us for their most important projects time and again.
